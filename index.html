Problem: current early-warning capability is 16–22% accurate, uses only structured data, and is fragmented across loan types. DefaultRadar fuses repayment behaviour, GST trend, Account-Aggregator bank-feed signals and NLP flags mined from field-visit notes into one probability-of-default score with a shared risk-decile interpretation across Term, Working-Capital, Mudra and Machinery loans.
